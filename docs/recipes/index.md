# Recipes

Recipes ready to drop into your Dingo cookbook. To add one, open the recipe and tap **Copy link**.
Then in Dingo, go to **Cookbook → Import**, paste the link and tap **Import**. You can check and edit everything before you save it.

!!! tip "New recipes added regularly"
    We add new recipes here regularly, so check back often for fresh ideas for your weekly menu.
