title: Weeknight Spaghetti Bolognese
description: A rich, slow-tasting meat sauce that simmers in under an hour, with a splash of milk for a silky finish.

category: Dinner
cuisine: Italian

prep_minutes: 15
cook_minutes: 60
total_minutes:
servings: 6

image:

ingredients:
  - 2 Tbsp olive oil
  - 1 onion, finely chopped
  - 1 carrot, finely chopped
  - 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 lb ground beef
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1/2 tsp black pepper
  - 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  - 1/2 cup dry red wine
  - 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - 1/2 cup milk
  - 1 lb spaghetti
  - grated Parmesan, for serving

steps:
  - name: Soften the vegetables
    text: Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot and celery and cook, stirring now and then, until soft, about 8 minutes. Stir in the garlic for 1 minute.
  - name: Brown the beef
    text: Raise the heat to medium-high, add the beef, salt and pepper, and cook, breaking it up, until no pink remains, 6 to 8 minutes.
  - name: Build the sauce
    text: Stir in the tomato paste for 1 minute, then pour in the wine and let it bubble until mostly gone. Add the crushed tomatoes and oregano.
  - name: Simmer
    text: Bring to a gentle bubble, then lower the heat, partly cover and cook for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the milk and cook 10 minutes more. Taste and add salt if needed.
  - name: Finish
    text: Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Drain, saving 1/2 cup of the pasta water. Toss the pasta with the sauce, adding a little pasta water to loosen it, and serve with Parmesan.
---

**Skip the wine:** use 1/2 cup of beef or chicken broth instead.

**Make ahead:** the sauce tastes even better the next day and freezes well for up to 3 months. Freeze it without the pasta.
