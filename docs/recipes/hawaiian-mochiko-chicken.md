title: Hawaiian Mochiko Chicken
description: Hawaii's favorite plate-lunch chicken, marinated in shoyu, garlic and ginger, then coated in sweet rice flour and fried until crisp.

category: Dinner
cuisine: Asian

prep_minutes: 20
cook_minutes: 25
total_minutes: 165               # includes 2 hours marinating
servings: 4

image:

ingredients:
  - 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  - 1/4 cup mochiko (sweet rice flour)
  - 1/4 cup cornstarch
  - 1/4 cup sugar
  - 1/4 cup soy sauce (shoyu)
  - 2 eggs, beaten
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  - 1/4 cup green onions, thinly sliced
  - 1/2 tsp salt
  - vegetable oil, for frying
  - steamed white rice, for serving

steps:
  - name: Make the batter
    text: In a large bowl, whisk together the mochiko, cornstarch, sugar, soy sauce, eggs, garlic, ginger, green onions and salt until smooth.
  - name: Marinate
    text: Add the chicken and stir until every piece is coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight for the best flavor.
  - name: Heat the oil
    text: Pour about 1 inch of oil into a heavy pot or deep skillet and heat it to 350°F.
  - name: Cook in batches
    text: Fry the chicken in batches, without crowding the pan, for 5 to 6 minutes, turning once, until deep golden and cooked through.
  - name: Drain and serve
    text: Drain on a rack or paper towels. Serve hot with steamed rice and macaroni salad for a classic plate lunch.
---

**Make ahead:** the overnight marinade gives the most flavor, so this is easy to start the night before.

**Air fryer:** shake off the extra batter, spray the chicken with oil and air fry at 380°F for 12 to 15 minutes, turning halfway through.

Look for mochiko (Koda Farms' blue-and-white box is the classic) in the Asian or baking aisle. Other sweet or glutinous rice flours work too.
