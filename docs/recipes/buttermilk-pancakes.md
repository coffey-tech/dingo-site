title: Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes
description: Tall, tender weekend pancakes from pantry staples, with a quick rest that makes them extra fluffy.

category: Breakfast
cuisine: American

prep_minutes: 10
cook_minutes: 20
total_minutes:
servings: 4

image:

ingredients:
  - 2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 2 Tbsp sugar
  - 2 tsp baking powder
  - 1/2 tsp baking soda
  - 1/2 tsp salt
  - 2 cups buttermilk
  - 2 eggs
  - 4 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted, plus more for the pan
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - maple syrup, for serving

steps:
  - name: Mix the dry ingredients
    text: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  - name: Combine
    text: In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla. Pour into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined. A few lumps are fine; overmixing makes pancakes tough.
  - name: Rest
    text: Let the batter sit for 5 to 10 minutes while the pan heats. It will thicken and turn bubbly.
  - name: Cook
    text: Heat a griddle or large skillet over medium heat and brush it with butter. Pour 1/3 cup of batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form on top and the edges look set, about 2 minutes, then cook 1 to 2 minutes more until golden.
  - name: Serve
    text: Keep finished pancakes warm on a baking sheet in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest. Serve with butter and maple syrup.
---

**No buttermilk?** Stir 2 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar into 2 cups of milk and let it stand for 5 minutes.

**Add-ins:** scatter blueberries or chocolate chips onto each pancake right after you pour the batter, before flipping.
