# Dingo is coming to the App Store

Dingo Meal Planner brings your recipes, weekly menu and grocery list together on your iPhone, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

<!-- more -->

Version 1.0 is on its way through App Store review. It includes:

- a weekly menu with breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks and dessert
- a cookbook with recipe import from most recipe websites
- grocery lists sorted by store department, with suggestions from the week's menu
- backups to Files or iCloud Drive

Questions or ideas? Email [support@dingo-meal-planner.com](mailto:support@dingo-meal-planner.com).
