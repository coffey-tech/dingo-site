# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Dingo Meal Planner ("Dingo") is a recipe, weekly menu and grocery list app. It is built so that your data stays on your device. This policy explains what that means.

## Data we collect

None. Dingo has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive or store any information about you or how you use the app.

## Data stored on your device

Your recipes, menus, grocery lists and app settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

## Recipe import

When you import a recipe from a web address, Dingo connects directly from your device to that website to download the page. The website receives an ordinary web request, as it would if you visited it in a browser, and its own privacy policy applies. Nothing about the request is sent to the developer.

## Backups

When you choose to back up your data, Dingo creates a backup file and hands it to the system share sheet or save dialog. You decide where it goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive or a message to yourself. Dingo doesn't upload backups anywhere on its own.

## Children

Dingo collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
