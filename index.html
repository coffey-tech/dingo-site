Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@dingo-meal-planner.com.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Nothing is sent to us, and deleting the app deletes its data.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and tap Back up now. Save the backup file to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself. On the new phone, install Dingo, open Settings, tap Restore from backup… and choose that file.

Why won't a recipe import from some websites?

Import reads the recipe data that most recipe sites publish. Some large sites block apps from reading their pages. For those, add the recipe by hand with Enter manually.
